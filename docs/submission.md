Title: **ProbeMinimax: Consensus adaptive diagnostic planner**

Notes / Description (under 1000 characters):

> ProbeMinimax compiles prose profiles into adaptive diagnostic policies with minimum worst-case cost. The contract fetches full commit-pinned documents and checks SHA-256. Validators independently derive every YES/NO/UNKNOWN outcome, require exact decision agreement and audit paragraph anchors against complete source text. UNKNOWN retains a profile on both branches. Exact minimax planning produces READY, COST_LIMIT or AMBIGUOUS with a shared-answer witness. Gasless StudioNet proofs cover adaptive cost savings, indistinguishable profiles, budget failure, resolvable UNKNOWN overlap and tampered-hash rejection with unchanged state. Four scenario writes finalized with MAJORITY_AGREE; receipts preserve dissent. The repo includes 45 direct tests, exhaustive checks over 810 ternary tables and 96 three-probe samples, and live-state verification. Synthetic profiles demonstrate closed-catalogue planning; costs are policy units and the contract does not execute probes or attest runtime behavior.
