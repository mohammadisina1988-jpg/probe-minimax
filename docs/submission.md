Title: **ProbeMinimax: Consensus adaptive diagnostic planner**

Draft description; live transaction links will be added after verification:

> ProbeMinimax compiles published prose profiles into an adaptive diagnostic policy with minimum worst-case cost. The contract fetches full commit-pinned documents and verifies SHA-256. Validators independently derive every YES/NO/UNKNOWN expected outcome, require exact decision agreement, and audit the leader's paragraph anchors against complete source text. UNKNOWN retains a profile on both branches. Exact minimax planning produces READY, COST_LIMIT, or AMBIGUOUS with an indistinguishable pair and shared-answer witness. A read-only walker exposes the next probe and candidate set for hypothetical answers. The repo includes 45 direct tests plus exhaustive checks over 810 ternary tables and 96 three-probe samples. Synthetic profiles demonstrate closed-catalogue planning; costs are policy units and the contract does not execute probes or attest runtime behavior.
